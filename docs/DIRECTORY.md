# Directory groups

The People page uses the site's B1 print style: paper, cobalt rules, orange accents and small colour portraits. Area sections contain organisations and independent practitioners. A parent-level Independent practitioners section repeats every independent profile in one place. Organisation sections contain profiles. Native disclosure controls work without JavaScript. Search, network and contact filters progressively enhance the complete HTML.

These are editorial navigation groups based on public work. They are neither exhaustive nor mutually exclusive. An organisation's function and an individual's specific work can differ. The directory labels this navigation “Browse by area”. Total counts deduplicate people and organisations.

## Independent practice and conversation routes

Affiliation answers where a person works. Independent practice answers whether their public work extends beyond that organisation. Conversation links answer how to approach them. Paid-work availability is a separate fact.

Alejo's [site](https://alejoacelas.com/) lists his 80,000 Hours contracting work, AI uplift work and a public calendar separately. Display him as an independent contractor, within both his current organisation and cross-cause practice. Do not publish a contract end date or time commitment without an explicit current public basis.

Peter's [consulting page](https://wow.pjh.is/consulting) invites email or a proposed call. Alexander provides his existing public appointment route. These three have prominent conversation links without a promise of free services or immediate capacity. The existing unknown or available work statuses remain visible. Ernesto's stated peer-exchange availability also matches the conversation filter.

The listing remains a public-source draft when the person has not approved it. No directory grouping or contact button grants consent on their behalf.
